After each successful passport registration, the smart contract emits a PassportIssued 
event containing the productId, IPFS CID, keccak256 metadata hash, issuer address, 
and block timestamp.

The productId and issuer fields are marked as indexed. In the EVM, indexed parameters 
are stored in the Bloom filter of the transaction receipt rather than in the standard 
ABI-encoded log data. This allows off-chain clients to efficiently filter logs by a 
specific product or manufacturer address without scanning the computational overhead 
of every block.

This design follows best practice for building queryable audit trails in Ethereum DApps. 
Any external service — a regulator dashboard, a compliance tool, or a manufacturer's 
own analytics — can subscribe to PassportIssued events and filter by issuer to retrieve 
a complete, tamper-evident history of every passport that wallet has ever registered, 
without requiring direct contract state queries.
